Best Cities in Bulgaria for Student Rental Investments: Sofia, Varna, or Plovdiv?

BinarySoul OP
07.02.2025 09:37
I am looking to buy a property specifically to target the student rental market in Bulgaria and I'm torn between Sofia, Varna, and Plovdiv. Sofia has the biggest universities and highest demand, but property prices are already quite steep. Varna and Plovdiv offer lower entry costs and strong tenant pools due to medical and technical universities, but I am unsure about the off-season liquidity. Which of these three cities offers the best balance of rental yield and capital appreciation for a foreign investor?
