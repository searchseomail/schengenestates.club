I inherited an apartment in Bansko a few years ago and I'm finally ready to sell it, but I live in the UK and cannot travel back to Bulgaria for the closing. What is the exact process for appointing a local lawyer via a Power of Attorney (PoA) to handle the sale on my behalf? Also, I would appreciate any advice on capital gains tax obligations and how to safely wire the sale proceeds back to my British bank account without losing a fortune on exchange rates.

Selling remotely from the UK is actually quite common for Bansko property owners. You'll need to draft a specific Power of Attorney (PoA), have it signed in front of a UK notary public, and then get an Apostille stamp attached. Your Bulgarian lawyer can then use this to sign the deeds at the notary in Bulgaria without you needing to fly over.

Regarding Capital Gains Tax (CGT), since you inherited the property, you need to check if you've owned it for more than 3 years. In Bulgaria, selling residential property held for more than 3 years is exempt from personal income tax. If it's been less than 3 years, the flat rate is 10% on the profit.
