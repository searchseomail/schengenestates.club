Directly paying a notary in Bitcoin or USDT is basically impossible in Bulgaria. Bulgarian property law requires all notarial deeds and official transactions to be settled in fiat currency (Bulgarian Lev or Euros) via bank transfer to create a verifiable paper trail for tax purposes.
