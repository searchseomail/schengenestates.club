Buying an Apartment in Nessebar Old Town vs New Town: Which Is Better for Year-Round Living?

OmicronDrift OP
04.02.2025 08:05
My spouse and I are planning to relocate to the Bulgarian coast and are currently looking at apartments in Nessebar, but we are completely torn between the Old Town and the New Town. We want a property that is comfortable for year-round living, meaning good access to grocery stores, medical facilities, and reliable heating during the winter months. While the Old Town has incredible charm and historic architecture, we are worried about parking issues, tourist crowds in the summer, and potential maintenance restrictions. On the other hand, the New Town seems more practical with modern infrastructure, but lacks the romantic seaside atmosphere. For those of you who live in Nessebar full-time, which area would you recommend for long-term comfort?
