Once you have the municipal registration number, make sure you hook up with a local accountant. You'll need to report your income monthly or quarterly and pay the flat 10% corporate tax (or 10% personal income tax after deductions, depending on whether you register as a sole trader or physical person).

The tourist tax (turisticheski danak) is paid monthly to the municipality based on the number of nights guests actually stayed. It varies slightly depending on the Sofia district, so check with your local tax office (Tergovska nadzir).
