OTP Bank and Fibank (First Investment Bank) are generally a bit more pragmatic than the others when it comes to non-residents buying real estate. You will need your valid passport, a signed preliminary purchase contract (notarized is better), proof of the origin of funds (like tax returns or bank statements from your home country), and a Bulgarian tax number (EGN or LNC) if possible.
