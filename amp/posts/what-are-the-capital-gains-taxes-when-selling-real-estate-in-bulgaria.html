I am a non-resident EU citizen considering selling an apartment I bought in Sofia a few years ago. As a non-resident EU citizen, you will generally be subject to a 10% flat capital gains tax on the profit made from selling the apartment in Sofia. The profit is calculated as the selling price minus the original purchase price and any documented improvement expenses.

You should definitely look into the holding period exemption. In Bulgaria, if you have owned the residential property for more than 3 years, the capital gains tax is 0% regardless of your residency status! If it's been less than 3 years, that 10% rate applies.
