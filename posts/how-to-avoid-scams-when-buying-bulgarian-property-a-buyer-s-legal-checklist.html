How to Avoid Scams When Buying Bulgarian Property: A Buyer's Legal Checklist

EchoRider
Topic Starter
Posted: 21.02.2025 12:19
I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday apartment on the Black Sea coast, but I've heard horror stories about double-selling and hidden debts tied to properties in Bulgaria. What are the essential legal steps and documents I need to verify before handing over any deposit? Can anyone recommend an independent lawyer who specializes in foreign property acquisitions there?

RogueVector
Replied: 02.05.2025 15:53
First and foremost, never pay a deposit without having an independent lawyer conduct a thorough background check at the Bulgarian Property Registry. Double-selling unfortunately happens when buyers rely on the seller's notary instead of hiring their own legal counsel.
AlphaProtocol
Replied: 25.05.2025 12:46
You should definitely read this comprehensive guide on buying property in Bulgaria, which breaks down the exact documents like the Act 16 and tax clearance certificates you need to demand.
ByteStorm

Make sure your lawyer checks for any hidden municipal debts or utility arrears. Developers sometimes pass on unpaid electricity and water bills to the new buyers.
QuantumQuill
Replied: 30.06.2025 10:14
When looking at the Black Sea coast, be extra careful with off-plan developments. Ensure the company actually owns the land and has valid building permits before signing anything.

Also, keep in mind that beyond the purchase price, you will have to deal with annual property taxes and maintenance fees, which can sometimes be surprisingly high in managed coastal complexes.
