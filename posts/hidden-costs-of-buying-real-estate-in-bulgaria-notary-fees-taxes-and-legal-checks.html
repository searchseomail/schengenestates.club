Hey everyone, I'm currently looking at apartments in Sofia and trying to put together a realistic budget. Beyond the asking price, what kind of hidden costs, notary fees, local taxes, and legal check expenses should I realistically expect to pay in Bulgaria? I want to make sure I don't get caught off guard by any unexpected closing costs during the purchase process.

Notary fees are actually regulated by law based on the property price, but they usually scale down percentage-wise the more expensive the property is.

Translation and interpretation fees! If your Bulgarian isn't fluent, you are legally required to have a sworn translator present at the notary, which costs around 100-200 euros.

Property Registry entry fee is usually around 0.1% of the property value, plus a small flat fee for issuing the final title deed extract.

Don't forget utility name transfer fees after you get the keys. It's not a huge amount, but water, electricity, and heating account transfers all add up to about 50-100 euros total.
