Investing in Sofia Real Estate: Rental Yields, Capital Growth, and Best Neighborhoods

RogueVector
Topic Starter
Posted: 15.01.2025 13:16
I am currently looking to diversify my portfolio and considering the Sofia property market for long-term buy-to-let investments. What are the current average rental yields I can expect in districts like Lozenets, Ivan Vazov, and Studentski Grad? Additionally, which areas are showing the strongest potential for capital appreciation over the next five years, and are there any specific regulatory pitfalls foreign investors should watch out for?

Lozenets and Ivan Vazov are fantastic for steady rental yields, usually hovering around 4% to 5.5%. Studentski Grad can give you higher yields, sometimes up to 7%, because of the constant demand from university students, but the tenant turnover is much higher.

If you are buying off-plan, make sure you thoroughly understand what are Act 14, Act 15, and Act 16 in Bulgarian real estate, as construction stages dictate your risk and financing options.

For capital appreciation, look at districts benefiting from ongoing metro line expansions, like parts of Ovcha Kupel or Krasno Selo. Established areas like Ivan Vazov already have high entry prices, so growth might be slower there.
