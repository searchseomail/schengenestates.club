Can Foreigners Buy Land in Bulgaria: Setting Up a Company vs Long-Term Lease

AlphaProtocol
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.02.2025 07:22
I am a non-EU citizen looking to acquire some agricultural and residential land in Bulgaria for a future retirement home and investment. I've read that non-EU nationals cannot directly own land in their own name, so I'm trying to figure out the best legal workaround. Would it be smarter and more cost-effective to set up a Bulgarian LTD (EOOD) company to purchase the property, or should I just look into a long-term lease agreement with an option to buy later? I would love to hear from expats or legal experts who have navigated this process recently.

As a non-EU citizen, you definitely cannot buy agricultural land directly in your own name, so setting up an EOOD is the standard route most people take. However, agricultural land has extra restrictions even for companies, so make sure to check out this Bulgarian property buying guide for a clear breakdown of the rules.
