SilentVortex
Topic Starter

Now that Bulgaria is officially in the Schengen area, I am seriously considering purchasing a holiday home on the Black Sea coast or near Sofia. I am wondering how this milestone has actually affected local real estate prices over the last few months. Additionally, does Schengen accession change any of the residency or visa rules for non-EU property buyers, or do the standard regulations still apply?

To answer your visa question: Schengen accession actually doesn't change the fundamental rules for non-EU citizens buying property. You still need a D-visa and subsequent residency permit if you plan to stay long-term. Property ownership alone doesn't automatically grant you a Schengen visa.
