How to Check a Bulgarian Property for Encumbrances and Outstanding Mortgages

ShadowMatrix
Topic Starter
Posted: 10.02.2025 15:23
I am currently in the process of negotiating a purchase for an apartment in Sofia, but I want to make sure there are no hidden debts or legal claims attached to it. What is the official procedure for checking a Bulgarian property for existing mortgages, възбрани (seizures), or other encumbrances? Can I do this online myself, or do I need to hire a local lawyer or notary to pull the Property Register certificate?

You can actually check some basic property details online through the Bulgarian Registry Agency portal, but for a foolproof official document showing mortgages and seizures, you really need a certified Property Status Certificate.

If you are buying in Sofia, do not rely solely on what the seller or broker shows you. Always get your lawyer or notary to pull the official certificate from the Property Register (Imoten registr).

A 'vazbrana' (seizure) or mortgage will show up clearly on the encumbrance certificate issued by the judge registry. Make sure your lawyer checks this right before the final preliminary contract and again before the final notary deed.
