Title Deeds in Bulgaria: How to Read and Verify Your Ownership Document

SolarPhantom
Topic Starter
Posted: 11.01.2025 04:56
I recently purchased a holiday apartment in Bansko and received the notary act (title deed), but I am struggling to understand all the sections and legal terminology inside. How can I independently verify that the property is fully registered in my name and check for any hidden encumbrances or mortgages? Are there specific online registries or local offices I need to visit to ensure my ownership is completely secure?

To check for encumbrances, you need to request a Certificate of Encumbrances (Удостоверение за тежести) from the Registry Agency. It costs a small state fee and shows any mortgages, liens, or claims against the property.
SleekPhantom
Replied: 02.04.2025 11:48
The notary act itself should state your name, personal ID number (EGN or LNC), and the exact description of the apartment, including the built-up area and ideal parts of the land. You can actually check the Property Register online through the Registry Agency portal (Aura system), but you will need a digital certificate or KES (qualified electronic signature) to log in and pull official documents.

Make sure you also register your new title deed with the local municipality (Obshtina) in Bansko within 2 months of the purchase to pay your local garbage and property taxes, otherwise you face a fine.
TitaniumMind
Replied: 18.11.2025 05:51
The Bulgarian system can be quite confusing with all the legal jargon, especially regarding ideal parts of the common areas. Hiring a local translator or independent lawyer for the first reading of the act is always a smart move.
RootAccess
Replied: 09.12.2025 05:25
Always keep your original notary act in a safe place. If you ever lose it, you can always obtain an official copy (prepis) from the notary who drafted the transaction or from the Registry Agency archives.
