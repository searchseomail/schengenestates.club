Buying Real Estate in Bulgaria as a UK Citizen: Post-Brexit Rules and 90-Day Limits

UrbanOracle
Topic Starter
Posted: 11.01.2025 16:25
I am a UK citizen currently looking into purchasing a holiday apartment on the Bulgarian coast, but I am very confused about how post-Brexit residency rules affect property ownership. Since we are now restricted to the 90/180-day Schengen rule as tourists, how does this impact the actual buying process and the time we can legally spend in our property each year? Are there any specific visa routes or residency permits tied to property ownership that would allow us to stay longer without triggering tax residency issues?

To stay longer than 90 days, some Brits look into the D-visa route, usually based on retirement, trade representative office status, or long-term medical treatment. However, maintaining a property alone isn't enough to secure a residency permit in Bulgaria anymore.

Keep in mind that spending more than 183 days a year in Bulgaria will automatically trigger tax residency there, meaning you'd owe taxes on your worldwide income. Most holiday homeowners just stick to the 90-day limit to avoid that massive headache.
