Negotiating Property Prices in Bulgaria: How Much Discount Can You Realistically Ask For?

CosmicCoffee
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.05.2025 01:34
I am currently looking to buy an apartment in Sofia and wondering how aggressive I can be with my initial offers. Are Bulgarian sellers generally open to negotiation, or is the asking price usually fixed? I would love to hear from expats or locals who have recently gone through the buying process about what percentage discount is actually realistic to achieve.

Replies (8)

UrbanOracle
Replied: 17.07.2025 06:43
In my experience buying a flat in Lozenets last year, sellers in Sofia are definitely open to negotiation, but asking for more than 10% right off the bat will usually just offend them. I managed to get about 6% off the asking price. Also, before you finalize, make sure you look into Bulgarian property insurance to protect your new investment.
ZenithCoder
Replied: 27.07.2025 12:24
It really depends on the condition of the property and how long it has been on the market. If it's a freshly renovated apartment in a prime location, the price is practically fixed. For older panel blocks needing work, you can push a bit more. Whatever you do, make sure you follow a proper legal checklist for buying Bulgarian property before handing over any deposits.
ByteStorm
Replied: 22.09.2025 08:37
A realistic discount in Sofia nowadays is usually between 3% and 7%. The market has been quite active, so sellers aren't generally desperate unless the property has some hidden flaws or has been listed for six months plus.
RogueVector
Replied: 09.12.2025 00:30
When I bought my place in Mladost, the initial listing was 115k euro, and we settled on 108k after some back and forth. So roughly a 6% discount. Don't be too aggressive or the agent won't even take your offer to the owner seriously.
TitaniumMind
Replied: 16.12.2025 11:05
Locals usually expect some room for bargaining, so asking prices are almost always padded by at least 5%. Start around 8-10% below asking, and you will likely meet somewhere in the middle around 5%.
BlazeRunner
Replied: 04.01.2026 10:27
Be very careful if you are looking at off-plan developments or properties that look half-finished. Read up on unfinished construction and abandoned developments in Bulgaria before making an offer, as negotiation dynamics are completely different there.
RetroRebel
Replied: 27.01.2026 06:03
As an expat who went through this in 2023, I learned that having cash ready gives you massive leverage. I got an 8% discount because I could close within three weeks without waiting for a mortgage approval.
ZenithCoder
Replied: 27.01.2026 08:28
The real estate agents in Sofia act as a massive buffer. They will usually tell you straight away if your opening offer is realistic for a specific neighborhood or if the seller is firm on their price.
