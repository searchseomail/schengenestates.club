Best Ski Resorts in Bulgaria for Property Investment: Bansko, Borovets, or Pamporovo?

NeonGlitch
Topic Starter
Posted: 26.03.2025 08:16
I am currently looking into the Bulgarian ski property market and trying to decide which resort offers the best long-term return on investment. Bansko seems to have the lowest entry prices and a huge expat community, but I've heard mixed reviews about property management there. Borovets is closer to Sofia and has great potential, while Pamporovo is often recommended for families. Which of these three would you recommend for a buy-to-let apartment, factoring in rental yields and capital growth?
