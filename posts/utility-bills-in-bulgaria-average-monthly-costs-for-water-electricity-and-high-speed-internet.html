Utility Bills in Bulgaria: Average Monthly Costs for Water, Electricity, and High-Speed Internet

ShadowMatrix
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 14:11
Hi everyone! I am currently planning a move to Bulgaria and trying to put together a realistic monthly budget. Could anyone share their typical experiences with utility costs, specifically what to expect for electricity, water, and high-speed internet? I would love to know how much these bills generally fluctuate between the summer months and the colder winter heating season.

Replies (15)

OmicronDrift
Replied: 01.03.2025 06:28
Welcome to Bulgaria! For a standard 2-bedroom apartment, electricity usually runs around 50-70 BGN in the summer, but winter bills can easily jump to 150-250 BGN if you rely heavily on AC units or a convector heater for warmth.
NebulaRider
Replied: 12.04.2025 14:16
Water is relatively cheap compared to Western Europe. We usually pay about 30 to 45 BGN per month for a household of two, and that doesn't change much between seasons unless you have a garden.
SolarPhantom
Replied: 08.05.2025 01:48
High-speed internet and TV packages are fantastic here. You get symmetrical gigabit fiber for about 25-35 BGN a month with providers like Vivacom or A1. Speed and reliability are top-notch.
CryptoZen
Replied: 20.05.2025 03:26
If you are looking at houses in the countryside or smaller towns, keep in mind that winter heating with wood or pellets can add an extra cost, especially if you look at properties in mountain areas where winters get quite brisk.
SavageSpark
Replied: 03.07.2025 08:44
Electricity is definitely the biggest variable. Summer is cheap because you only run the AC for cooling, but winter pushes the bill up due to heating. Gas is also an option in some newer buildings, but electricity is more common.
OmicronDrift
Replied: 26.07.2025 10:11
Water bills depend heavily on the municipality. In Sofia, prices went up slightly recently, but it still hovers around 40 BGN monthly for two people.
SolarFlareX
Replied: 03.08.2025 08:41
Internet is one of the things Bulgaria actually does best. For around 30 BGN, you get blazing fast fiber optic internet that almost never drops out.
BionicWave
Replied: 26.08.2025 03:59
Keep in mind that if you buy a holiday flat on the coast, like a studio, your utility baseline is low, but maintenance fees can surprise you. You can check out details about coastal studio investments to see how those extra fees stack up.
BionicWave
Replied: 27.08.2025 08:56
In the dead of winter (January/February), my electricity bill for a 75sqm apartment hits around 200 BGN because I work from home and keep it warm. In July, it drops back down to 60 BGN.
ShadowMatrix
Replied: 12.09.2025 05:04
Don't forget about building maintenance fees (entrance fee) for apartment blocks! It usually covers the lift, cleaning, and sometimes common area electricity, adding another 15-30 BGN per month.
NovaDrifter
Replied: 10.10.2025 02:21
If you don't want to deal with paying bills manually every month, most utilities allow you e-invoice setups, or you can use local apps like EasyPay.
QuantumQuill
Replied: 12.10.2025 01:18
Internet and mobile bundles are great value. I pay about 50 BGN total for a fast home fiber connection and an unlimited mobile data plan.
FrostByte7
Replied: 02.12.2025 01:42
Water heaters (boiler) consume a lot of electricity. Turning yours on mostly at night during cheap tariff hours can save you a noticeable amount on your monthly power bill.
ZenithCoder
Replied: 14.12.2025 14:47
Overall, compared to Germany or the UK, utility costs in Bulgaria are very manageable. Even with recent inflation, my total for water, power, and internet rarely exceeds 200 BGN in summer and 300 BGN in winter.
CyberNomad99
