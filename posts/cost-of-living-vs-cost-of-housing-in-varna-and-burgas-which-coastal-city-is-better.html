Cost of Living vs Cost of Housing in Varna and Burgas: Which Coastal City Is Better?

VelvetShadow
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.01.2025 13:09
Hey everyone, I'm planning to relocate to the Bulgarian Black Sea coast and I'm torn between Varna and Burgas. I keep reading mixed reviews about how utility bills, groceries, and especially real estate prices compare between the two cities. Could locals or expats who have lived in both places share their insights on which city offers a better financial balance? Specifically, how do current rental and property purchase prices weigh against general monthly living expenses?

Replies (12)

FrostByte7
Replied: 14.01.2025 13:21
I lived in Varna for three years and recently moved down to Burgas. Honestly, Varna feels a bit more vibrant and has a larger expat community, but it has definitely gotten pricier. Rent for a modern 2-bedroom in Varna is easily 10-15% higher than equivalent spots in Burgas.
RetroRebel
Replied: 10.02.2025 12:07
Burgas is much flatter and feels more compact, which I love because you can bike everywhere. When looking at property, don't forget to account for all the extra expenses. Before you sign anything, I strongly recommend reading this guide on hidden costs of buying real estate in Bulgaria so you aren't surprised by notary fees and local taxes.
VelvetShadow
Replied: 17.02.2025 14:42
Groceries and utilities are pretty much identical in both cities since they are both major Bulgarian hubs. The real difference is in the housing market. Varna's property prices are driven up by its status as the 'sea capital' and the university presence.
VortexSurfer
Replied: 28.02.2025 09:58
If you are planning to buy rather than rent, the process can be straightforward if you know what to expect. Make sure to check out buying property in Bulgaria as a foreigner to understand the legal nuances before committing your money to either Varna or Burgas.
SavageSpark
Replied: 10.05.2025 02:01
Both cities are great, but Burgas wins on urban planning. The Sea Garden in Burgas feels much cleaner and better maintained than Varna's, and the municipal administration seems very active. Rent-wise, you get slightly more square meters for your money in Burgas.
WiredNomad
Replied: 17.05.2025 12:09
Varna has better cultural events and bigger malls, but traffic in the summer is an absolute nightmare. Parking is also a massive headache compared to Burgas. If you want a more relaxed coastal lifestyle with reasonable living costs, Burgas takes the crown.
RogueVector
Replied: 02.07.2025 07:02
I compared both last year. Varna is definitely more expensive for dining out and entertainment. Burgas offers a slightly more authentic Bulgarian feel outside of the peak tourist months, and daily expenses like public transport and produce at the central markets are very cheap.
AstralApex
Replied: 22.09.2025 02:28
Some people get tempted by super cheap resort flats nearby, but that's usually a trap. You should read up on why apartments in Sunny Beach are so cheap before looking at holiday properties, as staying in Varna or Burgas year-round is a much safer bet.
NebulaRider
Replied: 03.11.2025 05:29
Utility bills in the winter can sting in older panel blocks in both cities. If you are renting or buying, make sure the place has proper insulation or air conditioning units that are efficient, otherwise your electricity bill will surprise you regardless of which city you pick.
BionicWave
Replied: 12.11.2025 01:16
Burgas has a great vibe and the expat community is growing steadily without feeling oversaturated. Real estate purchase prices per square meter are noticeably lower in Burgas neighborhoods like Lazur or Zornitsa compared to Varna's Tsumer or Chayka districts.
AstralApex
Replied: 31.12.2025 04:24
I work remotely and chose Varna because of the direct flight connections and the massive Sea Garden. Yes, rent is higher, but for me, the lifestyle tradeoff is worth it. If you are on a strict budget, though, Burgas is the smarter financial choice.
WiredNomad
Replied: 03.02.2026 13:57
At the end of the day, both cities offer an amazing Mediterranean-style life on a Black Sea budget. Varna leans slightly more expensive across all categories, while Burgas offers better value for your monthly living and housing expenses.
