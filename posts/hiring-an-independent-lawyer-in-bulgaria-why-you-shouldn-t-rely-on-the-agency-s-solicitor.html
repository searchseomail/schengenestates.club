Hiring an Independent Lawyer in Bulgaria: Why You Shouldn't Rely on the Agency's Solicitor

NebulaRider
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.01.2025 00:11
I'm currently in the process of buying a property in Sofia and the real estate agency keeps pushing me to use their in-house legal team to save time and money. However, a friend of mine warned me that this might be a conflict of interest since the agency ultimately represents the seller's interests as well. Has anyone gone through this recently, and is it really worth the extra cost to hire a completely independent Bulgarian attorney?
