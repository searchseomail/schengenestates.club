Reselling Bulgarian Property to Local Buyers vs Foreigners: Who Is Buying Right Now?

EchoChaser
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.01.2025 03:08
I've been trying to flip a two-bedroom apartment in Varna for the past six months with very little traction. Are local Bulgarian buyers stepping up to purchase resale properties, or are foreign investors still driving the current market? I'm trying to figure out where I should focus my marketing efforts and if I need to adjust my pricing strategy to appeal to a specific demographic. What are you guys experiencing on the ground right now?

Honestly, Varna is currently being driven almost entirely by local Bulgarian buyers upgrading their living space or investing local savings against inflation. Foreigners have cooled off significantly, especially for standard two-bedroom resales.

If you're stuck after six months, it's definitely a pricing issue, not just a marketing one. Locals are extremely price-sensitive right now. Have you checked out the buying property in Bulgaria as a foreigner guide? It breaks down how different demographics view property value differently.

Locals want turnkey properties with low maintenance overhead. If your apartment is in a gated complex with high yearly fees, you'll struggle because Bulgarians hate those extra costs.

Foreign buyers, particularly from Western Europe, are mostly looking at holiday villages or Sofia capital investments. Varna city center and suburbs are definitely a local game right now.

I flipped a flat in the Chaika district last year. Took about five months, but every single serious offer came from Bulgarian families using local bank mortgages. Focus your marketing in Bulgarian on local portals like Imot.bg.
