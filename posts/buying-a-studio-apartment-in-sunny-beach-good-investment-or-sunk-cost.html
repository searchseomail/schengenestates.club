Buying a Studio Apartment in Sunny Beach: Good Investment or Sunk Cost?

EchoRider
Topic Starter
Posted: 24.04.2025 13:16
I've been looking at some very cheap studio apartments in Sunny Beach, Bulgaria, and the potential rental yields look almost too good to be true. However, I've heard mixed reviews about maintenance fees, yearly depreciation, and the actual length of the tourist season there. Is buying a property in this resort town a smart move for passive income, or am I just setting myself up for a money pit?
