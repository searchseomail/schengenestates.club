Act 14, Act 15, and Act 16 in Bulgaria Explained: Why You Must Never Skip Them

NeonSpectre
Topic Starter
Posted: 29.01.2025 08:59
I am currently looking at buying an off-plan apartment in Sofia, and my agent keeps throwing around terms like Act 14, Act 15, and Act 16 without really explaining the legal weight behind each stage. I've heard horror stories about people losing money or being unable to move in because a developer skipped crucial paperwork or rushed the process. Could someone break down what each of these acts actually means and why it is an absolute dealbreaker to buy property before these milestones are properly certified?
