How to Get a Mortgage in Bulgaria as a Non-Resident: Banks, Rates, and Eligibility

NovaDrifter
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 13:06
I am a non-EU citizen looking to purchase a holiday apartment in Bansko and I'm wondering what the mortgage process is like for foreigners. Which Bulgarian banks are actually willing to lend to non-residents, and what kind of down payment and interest rates should I expect? I'd love to hear from anyone who has successfully gone through this process regarding the required documentation and eligibility criteria.

Replies (14)

UrbanOracle
Replied: 31.01.2025 16:21
Getting a mortgage in Bulgaria as a non-EU citizen is definitely possible, but it comes with stricter requirements. Most banks treat non-residents with caution, especially for resort areas like Bansko.
AsyncGhost
Replied: 13.02.2025 09:53
When I looked into this last year, Postbank and UniCredit Bulbank were the most open to lending to foreigners. However, be prepared for a much lower Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio compared to locals.
BlazeRunner
Replied: 05.03.2025 10:21
As a non-resident, expect to put down at least 40% to 50% as a down payment. Bulgarian banks rarely finance more than 50-60% for people without permanent residency or EU citizenship.
RootAccess
Replied: 14.03.2025 09:24
Interest rates for non-residents usually hover around 3.5% to 5.5% variable, depending on the bank and your financial profile. Make sure you read the fine print regarding EURIBOR shifts.
QuantumQuill
Replied: 30.04.2025 15:51
Before you even make an offer in Bansko, I highly recommend reading this detailed guide on buying property in Bulgaria as a foreigner to avoid common pitfalls with developers and title deeds.
GlitchArtist
Replied: 01.05.2025 00:43
The documentation list is extensive. You will need translated and apostilled proof of income, tax returns from your home country, bank statements for the last 6-12 months, and a clean criminal record.
FrostByte7
Replied: 12.05.2025 02:26
Keep in mind that Bansko apartments, particularly in older complexes, can sometimes be tricky to get valued properly by the bank's independent surveyor, which can affect your mortgage amount.
